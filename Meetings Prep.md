# Meetings Prep

Pairs with [Meetings](https://tao.rufuspollock.com/Meetings). How I prepare for and capture a meeting:

- **Keep meeting notes on Google Docs.** One doc per recurring meeting or topic, not scattered across tools -- easy to search back through later and easy to share.
- **Prepare your questions before the meeting, not during it.** Walk in with your questions already framed so the time gets spent on answers and discussion, not on working out what to ask.
- **Record the meeting.** You don't have to catch everything live if it's captured -- you can go back and check exactly what was said, not just what you remember.
- **Transcribe it with Granola.** Turns the recording into searchable text and a rough summary, so you're not the only person who can process what happened afterward.

Preparing questions in advance does the same job as [Hypotheses](https://tao.rufuspollock.com/Hypotheses): it forces you to think first, so the meeting sharpens your thinking rather than replacing it.
