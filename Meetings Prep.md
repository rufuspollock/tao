# Meetings Prep

Pairs with [Meetings](https://rufuspollock.com/tao/Meetings). How I prepare for and capture a meeting:

- Keep meeting notes on Google Docs.
- Prepare your questions before the meeting, not during it.
- Record the meeting.
- Transcribe it with Granola.

Preparing questions in advance does the same job as [Hypotheses](https://rufuspollock.com/tao/Hypotheses): it forces you to think first, so the meeting sharpens your thinking rather than replacing it.
