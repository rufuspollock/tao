# Information Efficiency

Every piece of communication -- an issue, a message, a meeting note -- costs someone else's time and attention to read. Write so that cost is as small as possible for the value delivered.

In practice:

- Front-load the key point rather than building up to it.
- Say the same thing in fewer words rather than more, unless the extra words are doing real work.
- Structure so a reader can skim and still get the gist -- headings, bold, short bullets over dense paragraphs.
- Don't make someone read the whole thread to find out what actually happened -- see [Issues](https://rufuspollock.com/tao/Issues) on keeping state, not just flow, up to date.

This is also why [Issues](https://rufuspollock.com/tao/Issues) need all the key information written into them directly, rather than scattered across comments someone has to piece together.
