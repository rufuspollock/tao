# Self-scoring and Review

How someone learns fast what "good" looks like without the reviewer checking everything forever.

1. **Each area has a kind of good:** one line for its goal and one for what good looks like.
2. **Score your own work first,** against that line, 1–10, with a sentence on why.
3. **AI reviews next** against the same line; only gaps go to the human reviewer.
4. **The reviewer responds only where their score differs.** The difference is the lesson.
5. **Review falls as scores agree,** per area: every piece → one in three → spot checks. Track where each area is.

Review the **shaping** (the [[Shaping Brief]]) more than the doing: that's where most rework starts.

Small rework is learning, not failure: log it with the lesson, and let it feed the next score.

Keep it a mirror, not a report: the person sees their own scores and data first.
