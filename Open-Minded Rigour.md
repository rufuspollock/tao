# Open-Minded Rigour

Pairs with [Hypotheses](https://rufuspollock.com/tao/Hypotheses): question everything, including yourself -- but not indiscriminately.

**Open-minded** means staying open to new approaches and ideas, and questioning received wisdom -- and, above all, questioning yourself ("Am I sure?"). One of the biggest traps we fall into as humans is getting attached to our views: we identify with them and hold on for dear life. Freedom and power come from holding views lightly.

But open-mindedness isn't "anything goes." Astrology and astronomy are not equally valid. Open-mindedness has to be paired with **rigour** and respect for expertise. Everything can be questioned -- but judiciously. Science and the scientific method are valuable and powerful; the master knows more than the novice. At the same time, what an expert knows isn't always easy to articulate, so intuition and judgment deserve respect alongside analysis and evidence. Measurement matters -- as long as you don't confuse the measure with the game.

Bring open-minded rigour to practical, intellectual and spiritual work alike.
