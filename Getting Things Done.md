# Getting Things Done

David Allen's system for personal task management: get everything out of your head and into a trusted system, so your mind is free to actually think instead of trying to remember.

The core loop:

- **Capture**: the moment something has your attention, write it down. Don't trust your head to hold it.
- **Clarify**: work out what it actually is and whether it's actionable. If not, it's reference material, someday/maybe, or trash.
- **Organize**: put it where it belongs -- next actions, a project list, a calendar, waiting-for.
- **Reflect**: review the system regularly so you trust it. An untrusted system gets ignored, and then you're back to holding everything in your head.
- **Engage**: with a trusted system in place, you can simply choose what to do next with confidence.

The point isn't the specific lists -- it's that your head is for having ideas, not holding them.
