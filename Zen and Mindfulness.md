# Zen and Mindfulness

Zen isn't a philosophy or a religion. It's the practice of seeing into the nature of your own being -- meditation, not doctrine. It tries to free the mind from the slavery of words and the constriction of logic, and points the way from bondage to freedom.

A few of the key pointers: you are not your thoughts. Be here now. Hold your views without attachment. Enlightenment is possible. There is no fixed self. Everything is interbeing.
