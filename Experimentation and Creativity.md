# Experimentation and Creativity

Stay open to experiment and change -- you don't have it all worked out, and you're not meant to.

As you explore new ground you have to embrace uncertainty. That doesn't mean deifying change for its own sake: be sensibly conservative *and* open-mindedly innovative at the same time. Treat your approach as something worked out on an ongoing basis, not a fixed plan to defend -- but don't confuse that with valuing change for the sake of change.
