# KISS Principle
Keep It Simple, Stupid -- see [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KISS_principle) for the fuller history and origin of the phrase.
Simple systems are easier to build, easier to explain, easier to fix, and easier for other people to actually use. Complexity is not a sign of sophistication -- it's usually a sign you haven't found the simple version yet.
When a solution feels complicated, that's a signal to go back and re-shape the problem rather than push through with more complexity. Add complexity only when the simple version has genuinely been tried and found wanting -- not in anticipation of needs that haven't shown up yet.
